Rules in plain English

The bot only “buys” with pretend money. A coin must pass all three checks:

  1. Volume spike — trading volume jumps to about 1.8× normal.
  2. News not bad — free headlines are checked; bad news (hack, ban, crash…) means skip.
  3. Near support — price is sitting just above a recent low point.

There are three separate fake wallets of $1,000 each: Stocks (big US stocks and ETFs like SPY, QQQ, AAPL, NVDA — trades only while the US market is open, 9:30–16:00 New York time), Big coins (BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP and other large coins) and Meme coins (meme + small coins). Each trade uses about 10% of that wallet's free cash, with a stop-loss about −3% and a take-profit up to +6%.
