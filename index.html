Rules in plain English

The bot only “buys” with pretend money. A coin must pass all three checks:

  1. Volume spike — trading volume jumps to about 1.8× its recent average (dead coins are skipped).
  2. News not bad — free RSS headlines are scanned; if the news looks negative (hack, ban, crash…), that coin is skipped.
  3. Near support — price is sitting near a recent swing-low support level.

Wallet starts at $1,000.00. Each new fake trade uses about 10% of free cash. It aims to take profit around +6% or cut the loss around −3%. Meme coins are included when they look liquid enough.

This is for learning only. Fake results are not a promise of real profit.
